## The error, named precisely

A **first floor door** is not a ground floor door. On the ground floor, a door can open onto grade, a plinth, or a verandah — fine. On the first floor, the moment you place a door in the external wall, the occupant steps out into **air**. If there is no balcony/landing/verandah outside it, you have drawn a *door to nowhere* — the classic drafting slip.

Two legal fixes, and only two:

1. **Add an external balcony/verandah** outside each such door (correct for habitable rooms — library, committee room, offices). This is what you want.
2. **Demote the door to a window** (correct only for non-access rooms — toilets, record stacks, shafts).

You picked option 1, so here's the corrected geometry.
